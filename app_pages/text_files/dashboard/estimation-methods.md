### Pedestrian Accidents - Indirect vs. Direct Estimation
    
There are two methods we use to get our crash rates. 
    
1. Direct Estimation: Uses a model specifically made for estimating pedestrian rates. This model takes in traffic volume alongside pedestrian volume and the number of lanes.
2. Indirect Estimation: Uses a model for vehicle accident rates and assumes pedestrian crashes will make up around 2\% of those crashes. 

This difference is important specifically for the 4th & Agate intersection, as it is the only intersection where we can leverage direct estimation. This is because that kind of estimation is only an option for **signalized** intersections. Not only does this mean that it can't be used for Mesa, it also means this kind of estimation isn't available for 4th *until it's been signalized*. Thus, we only provide it as an alternative result for the after scenario.

Due to this, caution is recommended when comparing the pedestrian rates between direct and indirect scenarios. The direct pedestrian estimations seem like a drastic safety increase, but it's hard to say what the before scenarios would show if they had access to direct estimation as well.
