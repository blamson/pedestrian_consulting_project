Minor Road Traffic Percent
- Measure of how close minor road volume is to Agate ave volume. 
- Is multiplied by AADT Major to get AADT Minor.
- Both this value and AADT Major determine AADT Minor.
- Example: 10% would mean minor road volume is set to 10% of major road volume. 10,000 * 10% = 1,000
- Default values taken from SGM observations.
- May cause ADDT Minor to exceed intended model limits. This is okay.
