Bulbout
- Also known as a curb extension.
- Extends width of curb or sidewalk into the road, narrowing roadway and increasing pedestrian visibility.
- CMF Value: 0.63. Reduces crashes by 37%.
- CMF Clearinghouse ID: 1786
- Crash Types: Pedestrian
